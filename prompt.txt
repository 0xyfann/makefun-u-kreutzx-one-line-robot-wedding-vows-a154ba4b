Write one funny single-line wedding vow for a robot couple, 1-160 characters.

Judge criteria (most important first):
1. It must be a one-line robot wedding vow that lands as a joke.
2. The laugh is in the line itself: a real turn, a surprise, or wordplay that
   pays off in the last few words.
3. It sounds like something said at a wedding, with a promise or commitment.
4. The machine side is specific: sensors, firmware, batteries, updates,
   backups or ports — not a joke that would work for any couple.
5. It fits one line and reads well aloud, with a rhythm that lets the laugh land.

Approach: open with a classic wedding promise ("I promise to love you
through..."), stack two specific robot hardships (firmware updates, dead
batteries), then land the surprise in the last words — being called by an
ex's serial number, a robot-flavoured version of the classic "wrong name"
wedding joke.
